RSVP Reader: privacy

Last changed 25 November 2026.

RSVP Reader is a reading app that runs on your own device. It has no server and no accounts, and its developer receives nothing from it: no books, no reading history, no usage statistics, no crash reports.

What stays on your device

Your books, your place in each one, your notes, bookmarks, shelves and settings are kept in the app's own storage on the device you are using. The app's error log is kept there too, and is only ever shown to you. Removing the app, or clearing its data, removes all of it.

Google Drive sync, if you turn it on

Sync is off until you press Connect in Settings. If you connect, you sign in with Google and the app is given one permission: to see and manage the files it creates itself in your Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

With that permission the app keeps a folder named "RSVP Reader" in your own Drive. It holds your books, your notes, your reading positions, bookmarks, shelves and settings, and a name for each device you have connected (such as "Firefox on Linux"), so that your other devices can read them. This information goes from your device to your Google Drive and back. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or for anything other than keeping your devices in step.

What is in your Drive is held by Google under Google's privacy policy. The sign-in that lets the app reach your Drive is kept on your device.

RSVP Reader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping sync and deleting what it stored

  • Press Disconnect in Settings to stop syncing on a device.
  • Delete the "RSVP Reader" folder in your Google Drive to delete everything sync stored there.
  • Remove the app's access at any time under Third-party apps with account access in your Google Account.

Questions

The app is open source. Its code, and a place to ask questions, is at github.com/HawkOsm/rsvp-reader.
